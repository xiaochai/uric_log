隐私政策

尿酸记录 App

尿酸记录采用本地优先设计。您的尿酸数值、备注等健康内容保存在设备本地；仅当您主动开启 iCloud 同步时，相关数据才会通过 Apple CloudKit 在您自己的设备间同步。

1. 我们处理的信息

我们不会向产品分析或广告服务上传尿酸检测数值、目标数值、测量日期、记录备注、性别设置或其他健康内容。

为了了解功能使用情况并改进应用，我们通过 PostHog 处理有限的产品分析信息，包括：

  • 应用启动、页面切换、按钮点击、功能开关及筛选范围等交互事件；
  • 应用版本、操作系统版本、设备类型、应用语言和时区等基础技术信息；
  • PostHog 生成的随机标识符，以及网络请求过程中可能处理的 IP 地址。

我们不要求您为产品分析提供姓名、邮箱或电话号码，也不会主动将分析标识符与您的健康记录关联。

2. 本地存储与 iCloud

  • 本地存储：健康记录首先保存在设备本地数据库中。
  • iCloud 同步：开启后，记录会通过 Apple CloudKit 存储在您的私人 iCloud 账户并在您的 Apple 设备间同步。我们无法直接查看您的私人 CloudKit 数据。
  • 关闭同步：您可随时在应用设置中关闭 iCloud 同步。

3. CSV 导出

您可以将记录导出为 CSV 文件。文件仅保存或分享至您主动选择的位置，应用不会代您将导出文件上传至我们的服务器。

4. 产品分析

我们使用 PostHog 统计匿名化的产品使用趋势、验证安装和协助排查问题。分析服务不受广告授权选择影响。

  • 分析数据可能由 PostHog 的美国区域服务处理；
  • 我们关闭了 Session Replay、控件自动采集、自动页面采集、问卷、推送订阅采集和崩溃自动采集；
  • PostHog 隐私政策。

5. 广告与授权

应用根据下载账号对应的 App Store 地区选择广告服务，并在完成适用的隐私告知或选择流程后请求广告：

  • 中国大陆 App Store：使用友盟 U-AppWin。首次使用时由应用展示广告隐私授权提示。
  • 其他 App Store 地区：使用 Google AdMob，并通过 Google User Messaging Platform（UMP）根据当地法规判断是否需要征求同意或提供隐私选项。
  • 广告服务可能处理设备信息、广告标识符、IP 地址、网络信息及广告互动信息，但不会接收您的尿酸记录或备注。
  • 友盟隐私政策；Google 隐私政策。

6. 数据保留与安全

本地数据由您控制，可在应用内删除；卸载应用会删除设备上的本地数据。iCloud 数据由您的 Apple 账户管理。第三方服务处理的信息按照其配置、合同及隐私政策保留。数据传输使用 HTTPS 等行业通行的安全措施。

7. 您的选择与权利

  • 查看、添加、修改、删除或导出自己的记录；
  • 开启或关闭 iCloud 同步；

本应用不面向儿童提供定向服务，也不会明知收集儿童的健康记录或直接身份信息。如您认为儿童向我们提供了相关信息，请联系我们。

9. 政策更新

Privacy Policy

Uric Acid Log

Uric Acid Log is designed to keep your health records local. Uric acid readings, notes, and related health content remain on your device. They are synchronized through Apple CloudKit only when you enable iCloud sync.

1. Information We Process

We do not send uric acid readings, target values, measurement dates, record notes, gender settings, or other health content to analytics or advertising services.

To understand feature usage and improve the app, we process limited product analytics through PostHog, including:

  • App launches, screen navigation, button taps, feature settings, and filter selections;
  • Basic technical information such as app version, operating system version, device type, app language, and time zone;
  • A random identifier generated by PostHog and the IP address that may be processed while handling network requests.

We do not require your name, email address, or phone number for product analytics, and we do not intentionally associate analytics identifiers with your health records.

2. Local Storage and iCloud

  • Local storage: Health records are stored in the app database on your device.
  • iCloud sync: When enabled, records are stored through Apple CloudKit in your private iCloud account and synchronized between your Apple devices. We cannot directly access your private CloudKit data.
  • Disabling sync: You can turn off iCloud sync in the app settings at any time.

3. CSV Export

You can export records as a CSV file. The file is saved or shared only to a location you select, and the app does not upload exported files to our servers.

4. Product Analytics

We use PostHog to understand aggregated product usage, verify installations, and troubleshoot problems. Product analytics is not controlled by your advertising consent choice.

  • Analytics data may be processed by PostHog services in the United States;
  • Session Replay, automatic element capture, automatic screen capture, surveys, push subscription capture, and automatic error tracking are disabled;
  • PostHog Privacy Policy.

5. Advertising and Consent

The advertising service is selected using the App Store region associated with the account that downloaded the app. Ad requests begin only after the applicable privacy notice or choice flow is completed:

  • China mainland App Store: Umeng U-AppWin is used. The app presents an advertising privacy notice before initialization.
  • Other App Store regions: Google AdMob is used. Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) determines whether consent or privacy choices are required under local law.
  • Advertising services may process device information, advertising identifiers, IP addresses, network information, and ad interactions. They do not receive your uric acid records or notes.
  • Umeng Privacy Policy; Google Privacy Policy.

6. Retention and Security

You control locally stored records and can delete them in the app. Uninstalling the app removes its local data. iCloud data is managed through your Apple account. Information processed by third-party services is retained according to their configuration, agreements, and privacy policies. Industry-standard safeguards such as HTTPS are used for data transmission.

7. Your Choices and Rights

  • View, add, edit, delete, or export your records;
  • Enable or disable iCloud sync;
  • In China mainland, decline advertising consent. Local record features remain available and the Umeng advertising SDK is not initialized;
  • In other regions, use Google's privacy options to manage advertising consent. Google may provide limited or non-personalized ads depending on your choices and applicable law;
  • Contact us to ask questions or exercise rights relating to analytics information.

8. Children's Privacy

The app does not provide services targeted to children, and we do not knowingly collect children's health records or direct identifiers. Contact us if you believe a child has provided such information.

9. Policy Updates

We may update this policy when features, service providers, or legal requirements change. Material changes will be communicated through the app or this page.

10. Contact Us

For privacy questions, contact lq.so@qq.com.
